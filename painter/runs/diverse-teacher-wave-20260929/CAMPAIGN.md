# Diverse text/photo teacher wave, 2026-09-29

**Hypothesis.** Broader, source-disjoint teacher trajectories will give the next SFT/RL curriculum more varied spatial structures than the 600 raw first-paint candidates and 100 repair scenes. This is data generation, not evidence that the current student improved. The matched student baseline for any later training decision is the public Qwen3.8-27B step-512 photo-SFT adapter, with the current text-RL continuation and its frozen 47-brief validation kept separate.

The source manifest has **120 new task inputs**: 60 authored text briefs over ten subject families and 60 human-screened COCO val2017 photos over ten visual families. The photo selection came from a 96-image contact sheet and excludes empty/sign-only or unclear views. All photos are from the already pinned 250-train-photo pool; each row carries exact bytes/hash, original source URL, and COCO license metadata. The 60 selected photo IDs had zero overlap with the 66 prior local teacher source archives scanned and their 71 COCO val IDs. The text briefs are exact-string disjoint from prior local source manifests. Deterministic source archive SHA-256 `11dc6f4d819d558a4799594e8b6e636e6bc4027cfbe01c79f44bcb59f1726fcb`, manifest SHA-256 `d44cde3a4ddf55a21832c35558055dac2951685bde289ecda6c35993ce7cd9f9`.

The [public source archive](https://huggingface.co/datasets/CK0607/komorebi-painter-teachers/blob/a46af0ee53490c6fcf2d20d806eb3b93861d074d/curricula/diverse-teacher-wave-20260929/source.tar.gz) was anonymously downloaded and hash-verified at revision `a46af0ee53490c6fcf2d20d806eb3b93861d074d`. Only the source images and manifest are public so far; no teacher output has yet been generated.

The finite CPU-only plan is 12 shards of 2–12 tasks each. MiMo v2.6 Flash handles 76 simpler inputs and MiMo v2.6 Pro handles 44 harder inputs, assigned before output review. Each episode has at most four render→inspect→revise turns and a 2,400-second episode deadline. One canary episode must render a valid canvas and publish a hash-verified public result before the shard continues. Then each shard publishes prefixes after four, eight, and all episodes. At maximum the wave makes 480 Gateway requests; actual requests, reported tokens, and provider cost fields must come from result receipts. Missing cost fields mean unknown cost, not zero. No GPU node or recurring watcher is used for this wave.

The existing `p5.brush` rendering contract, 600×600 WEBGL setup, and teacher prompt remain the basis. The runner now accepts a hash-bound text-only brief with **no reference-image content part**; subsequent text turns see only the rendered canvas and original brief. Image turns retain exact photo plus current canvas. Local text/photo dry runs passed before paid calls. The runner's full unit suite has two pre-existing fixture errors because the cloned benchmark directory lacks its ignored JPEG; the new focused text-only test passes.

**Admission rule.** Teacher outputs are candidates. Preserve every turn, program, canvas, renderer error, and finish action. Visually compare each candidate to its brief or actual photo; admit only source-faithful, attractive, renderer-valid turns and genuine improvements over the preceding canvas. Do not train on final-turn code automatically or count a new revision as a new scene. Report candidate count, valid count, visually admitted count, and text/photo family coverage separately. Revise failed categories, not the active RL reward, during this run.

The ongoing 50-step text-RL run is independent. After it stops, profile measured phase times and failure classes, inspect rollout galleries, evaluate the final public checkpoint against both frozen SFT and step-16 on the 47 held-out briefs, and publish the evidence before releasing the GPU node. Do not infer student improvement from online reward alone.
