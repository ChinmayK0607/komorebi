## Curriculum and controller

The generated [curriculum manifest](curriculum-manifest.json) retains the prior 96 training rows and 32 heldout rows by source-group, prompt hash, family, stage, and variant. Four stages run in order: single forms (4 sampling cycles), connected objects (4), spatial pairs (8), and scenes (4). Every sampling cycle schedules six Prime task groups, one prompt row from each of six training families. Each task group dispatches four rollouts, giving a 24-trace capacity per cycle; actual scored exposure is measured after admission because a group may contribute three traces when one rollout is judge-uncertain. Prime's `batch_size=24` counts trainable traces and may span multiple sampling cycles, so optimizer update receipts are recorded independently. Each family's four variants cycle deterministically. The 32 heldout rows are one fixed panel for the exact start and final policy.

The [controller](staged_curriculum.py) emits explicit pass/stop/advance receipts. A model-owned invalid code, canvas, current-canvas, or terminal action is recorded as a trainable reward-zero rollout and does not halt the schedule. Judge uncertainty or service failure is recorded as an excluded, unscored rollout with evidence; a group is admitted with three or four scored traces and censored below three. Native dispatch failures and non-stale cancellations become explicit service-owned unscored placeholders; stale cancellation censors the cycle as a pipeline decision. Missing or malformed ownership, status, evidence, validity, reward, row or family, duplicate group, wrong cardinality, or quota mismatch returns `status=stop`, `decision=stop`, and `admission=fail_closed`. A pass receipt advances only the sampling cycle. The `4/4/8/4` optimizer target receipts advance stages; this is schedule progression rather than a quality gate. Each stage has a finite two-times sampling-attempt cap, including censored retries, and stops if its optimizer target cannot be filled. State does not advance after a censored or rejected cycle. The controller therefore records an actual staged protocol rather than labeling ordinary cyclic mixed sampling a curriculum.
