## Result and decision

The trainer exited 0 after all 160 updates. Training loss was finite (0.574 at step 1, 0.149 at step 160); that is an optimization check, not a visual-quality measure. Every checkpoint is in the **public** [model repository](https://huggingface.co/CK0607/qwen3.8-27b-brush-painting/tree/752227cef80d1d3903a38242512e2bfc6a32accb/brush-sft-20260927-v1) and its weight hash was verified independently with anonymous Hugging Face metadata: step 40 `c29512fdad654171e148971babc593789f5a13f801da6304538a89ca27ec0190`, step 80 `0101b51ab7f6dff25f5f14b597caf4eff7177e5d6dd1c26e335b3d4062463ff3`, step 120 `cc3a1af876696fc20a10236cf57b8e6d2355cc7e41d89615634f1a8351ba0d46`, and step 160 `e94c08b6441ad76984082c6374ce1b970bed10e5bfe7eff19a83ebd018122705`. Final checkpoint commit: `752227cef80d1d3903a38242512e2bfc6a32accb`.

This is a modest validity gain, not a reliable beauty gain. **Evaluation limitation:** The 16,384-token vLLM context plus a fixed request for 8,192 output tokens caused second-turn HTTP 400 context errors on 1, 2, and 4 cases respectively. These are infrastructure-censored revision opportunities, not failed painting programs. The baseline also produced an unintended duplicate evaluation pass; the first pass covered all 28 cases with no episode errors, so the second pass was stopped after two traces and preserved separately. The post-run launcher now defaults to 32,768 context tokens and skips an already completed matched evaluation; this mitigation is syntax-checked but **has not been GPU-validated**. A definitive multi-turn ranking requires a corrected matched evaluation. The first-turn validity result, which does not depend on the second-call bug, is essentially flat.

Training-window telemetry sampled both GPUs every ten seconds from `2026-09-27T07:56:24Z` to `08:44:49Z`: GPU 0 (trainer) averaged 73.4% utilization and was ≥10% busy in 260/290 samples; GPU 1 (concurrent baseline inference) averaged 91.5% and was ≥10% busy in 267/290 samples. The denominator is all parseable per-GPU samples during that window, not the full rental. GPU 1 became idle after its midpoint evaluation while GPU 0 finished the final evaluation; the full pipeline was therefore not continuously saturated. The two-GPU node was rented for about 1.9 hours at the listed $2.38/hour and showed $4.47 spent immediately before release. It was removed after the public checkpoints and evidence bundle were hash-verified; no GPU pods remained active.

**Next decision:** Do not stack RL on step 160 or call this a successful quality improvement. For the next quality-first experiment, use the retained high-quality renderable demonstrations with stronger reference-fidelity selection and less homogenizing teacher style, fix the multi-turn context budget, and run a small matched first-paint plus revision evaluation before spending on another long training run. Step 40 remains an available public candidate if corrected pairwise evidence later favors it.
