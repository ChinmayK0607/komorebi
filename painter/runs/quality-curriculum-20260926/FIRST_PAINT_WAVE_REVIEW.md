# Diverse first-paint wave: visual screening

The purpose of this wave is to find *correction states*, not to equate renderer success with training quality. Every visual observation below is on an identified, nonblind reference/canvas or prompt/canvas pair. The matched baseline for a future correction is the exact first canvas in the same episode. No new first paint is admitted to the high-quality SFT export by this record.

An independent Luna-low agent inspected each exact reference and canvas and saved a hash-checked [raw visual audit](../../collected/quality-curriculum-20260926/luna-simple-v2-audit.json). The audit used `admit` for *correction-shortlist eligibility*, not an SFT-quality admission. Parent inspection of a 12-pair contact sheet and selected full-size pairs applies a stricter beauty/fidelity bar: **zero of these 12 first paints are admitted directly**. The rabbit is a faceted caricature with a scratched-over body; the camera's decorative gold blobs replace the actual background and its body/lens proportions are off; the honey-toast still life loses the knife/toast relationship in scratch lines. The clouded lake, tundra shack, bottles and other examples retain recognizable layouts but rely on flat repeated marks. The three-horse painting loses the overlapping anatomy entirely. The antique camera and man-at-desk scene are chosen only as diverse *observed-canvas correction seeds* for a later render-and-compare pass.

The [local review gallery](../../collected/quality-curriculum-20260926/rendered-cloud/teacher500-sol-simple-cc0-v2-20260926/review.html) preserves the reference and canvas. ## Simple CC0 photo wave, selection v1

The 16-photo `sol-simple-cc0-select-v1` run produced 15 valid canvases and one **censored timeout** at the 600-second per-program deadline. It took 1,006 seconds wall time on two Linux CPU render workers. The timed-out sunflower-field episode has no observed canvas; it is neither a visual success nor a malformed-program proof. The [local gallery](../../collected/quality-curriculum-20260926/rendered-cloud/teacher500-sol-simple-cc0-v1-20260926/review.html) and summary preserve the evidence. Luna-low inspected all 16 exact photo/canvas pairs where a canvas exists and its parent-verified [audit](../../collected/quality-curriculum-20260926/luna-simple-v1-audit.json) records each input/output hash. The strict visual screening admitted **0/15** first paints: the recognizable train, butterfly, lighthouse, can and other subjects remain flat, anatomically or compositionally inaccurate, or covered in stray lines. Parent inspection of a 16-pair contact sheet agrees. Lighthouse, watering can and clock were nominated as correction seeds; a separate Astra-high correction began on lighthouse and ladybug to probe both a scene and small object. Their authored code is not yet a better painting.

Two disjoint Luna-low audits inspected all 20 prompt/canvas pairs; their normalized, parent-verified copies are under `painter/collected/quality-curriculum-20260926/luna-text-curated-{a,b}-audit.json`. One raw audit had a prompt-hash typo, preserved and disclosed in the normalized copy rather than silently rewritten.

Luna nominated caramel custard, copper-eave icicles and two baklava diamonds as higher-tier. Parent full-size inspection found the custard a plausible stylized correction seed, though it is too pale and its amber top lacks gloss. The icicles lack the prompt's warm weathered copper, and the baklava pieces are rectangles rather than diamonds. These are **not** three confirmed high-quality admissions. The custard alone is queued for a visually inspected next-turn correction; admission depends on the actual rendered comparison. The [local text gallery](../../collected/quality-curriculum-20260926/rendered-cloud/teacher500-astra-text-curated-20260926/review.html) and run summary preserve the exact outputs.

## Decision

Continue with the 16-photo v1 render collection and the six already-authored actual-canvas corrections. Render the newly selected camera, desk and custard corrections only after their evidence manifests pass hash/contract checks. Preserve failed or mixed cases as negatives/diagnostics; promote only images that are visibly better on structure, fidelity and beauty. This screening is not evidence that a student improved, and no GPU training is launched from these candidates.
